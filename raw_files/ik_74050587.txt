3. Learned counsel for the applicant has submitted that his client has been falsely implicated in case crime no. 01 of 2023, P.S. Cyber Crime for an offence under Section 420 IPC and Section 66D of I.T. Act, who is languishing in jail since 11th April, 2025. Further contended that the first information report is delayed, the amount transferred to the account of accused-applicant has been credited to the account of co-accused Vikas Chaurasia; the charge sheet has been submitted in the matter and co-accused Vikas Chaurasia has already been enlarged on bail, who has a major role in the present matter.

4. Learned AGA has opposed the prayer for bail and submitted that the accused-applicant is having criminal history, who has other cases as well registered against him. The matter pertains to cyber fraud, wherein the informant Anila Sahu has been duped of Rs. 6,50,000/- by transferring the money by way of cheating from the mobile phone of informant to the bank account of present accused-applicant by playing fraud on her. It is further submitted that the present accused has in turn transferred the amount to the account of co-accused Vikas Chaurasia. As such, it is prayed that the accused-applicant shall not be enlarged on bail.

7. On three dates, viz 3rd, 4th and 5th September, 2023 certain amounts have been transferred from the account of informant to the account of present accused-applicant, who has transferred the same to the account of co-accused Vikas Chaurasia. The co-accused Vikas Chaurasia has already been enlarged on bail by the co-ordinate Bench of this Court in Crl. Misc. Bail Application No. 14217 of 2024 vide order dated 22nd April, 2024. It is apparent on the face of the record that the amount which has been credited to the account of Rahul Soni, the present accused-applicant has further been transferred to the account of Vikas Chaurasia. The charge sheet has already been submitted and the accused applicant is languishing in jail since 11th April, 2025.
